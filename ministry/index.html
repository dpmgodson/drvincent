Why this page exists

Not everything that shapes a person belongs in a curriculum vitae.

My work in ministry has deepened my understanding of leadership as service. It has reinforced my conviction that knowledge can develop the mind, while service is what reaches people and communities.

I teach in church as I teach in a lecture hall: by preparing properly, saying what I can support, and leaving room for the people listening to think. The subject differs. The obligation does not.

This page is here because leaving it out would misrepresent how I actually work. It is not here to persuade anyone of anything.

What it has changed

Three things I would not have understood otherwise.

That leadership is mostly maintenance. The visible part of leading is decision-making. The actual part is showing up consistently for work nobody is measuring — which is also, it turns out, what institutional quality assurance is.

That teaching is pastoral whether or not you intend it. A student struggling with structural analysis is rarely only struggling with structural analysis. Recognising that changed how I supervise.

That competence without purpose is unstable. Engineering can make something stand up. It cannot tell you what is worth building. I have needed an answer to that from somewhere.

Knowledge develops the mind. Service reaches the person.
