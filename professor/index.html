Academia

I teach the fundamentals because new technology still depends on them.

My teaching is centred on structural engineering and mechanics. My research gives those fundamentals a place to be tested against current construction problems.

Teaching

Structural Analysis. Strength of Materials. Mechanics of Solids.

These subjects form much of my teaching work at Karunya. I also teach Advanced Structural Analysis and related civil engineering subjects.

I see teaching and research as connected. Students need the fundamentals to understand why a structure behaves as it does; research gives them examples where the answer is not already in a textbook.
