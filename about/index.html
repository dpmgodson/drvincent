About me

I became a teacher before I became a researcher with a large research portfolio.

My career has grown gradually: civil engineering education, teaching, doctoral research, structural engineering research, supervision and, more recently, institution relations.

My beginning

Civil engineering gave me a practical way to think.

I completed my B.E. in Civil Engineering in 2006 and M.Tech. in Structural Engineering in 2008. I began teaching soon after, and teaching remained a constant even as my research developed.

My doctoral work at Karunya examined confinement and skin reinforcement of masonry infills in reinforced-concrete moment-resisting frames. The work involved experimental and analytical study of a problem that is easy to overlook in design, but important to how buildings behave during lateral loading.
