Standard

This site is built to target WCAG 2.2 Level AA. That means, among other things: text contrast of at least 4.5:1, every function reachable by keyboard, visible focus indicators, semantic headings in order, labelled form controls, and respect for the operating system's reduced-motion setting.

What has been done

  • {{icon:check}}A skip link to main content on every page.
  • {{icon:check}}Landmark regions and a single H1 per page.
  • {{icon:check}}Colour contrast checked against AA for every text and background pair in use.
  • {{icon:check}}Full keyboard operation of navigation, filters and search, with visible focus.
  • {{icon:check}}Animation and smooth scrolling disabled when prefers-reduced-motion is set.
  • {{icon:check}}Text alternatives on informative images; decorative images marked as such.

Known limitations

Some photographs on this site are placeholders pending authentic photography, and their descriptions will be revised when the final images are in place.
