Quality assurance

NAAC A++, CGPA 3.53.

As IQAC Coordinator, he contributed to the institutional quality-assurance work that culminated in NAAC A++ accreditation with a CGPA of 3.53 for Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences.

Accreditation at that level is not won by a submission. It is won by years of process: evidence gathered consistently across departments, criteria mapped to practice rather than to aspiration, and documentation maintained by people who will never be named in the result. The coordinator's job is to make that routine.

The same work continues outside the accreditation cycle — in admissions, in institution relations, in school outreach and in academic engagement across the University.
