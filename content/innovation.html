A pattern in the work

The patents came out of the laboratory, not out of a patent strategy.

Each of the inventions below started as an obstruction in an experiment. Reinforcement congestion at a joint made a specimen hard to cast. A printable mix would flow or hold its shape but not both. A printer could deposit concrete but could not reinforce it.

Intellectual property, in this account, is what happens when a workaround turns out to be general.
