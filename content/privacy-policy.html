The short version

This is a static personal website. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no tracking pixels, and has no server-side code that could store anything about you.

What is not collected

No accounts, no forms that submit to a server, no newsletter list, no advertising identifiers, no session recording. There is nothing here to opt out of.

Third-party requests

Two external services are contacted by your browser when a page loads:

  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Google receives your IP address as part of that request.
  • Unsplash serves some photographs. Unsplash receives your IP address as part of that request.

Links to ORCID, Scopus, Google Scholar, ResearchGate, LinkedIn, DOI resolvers and publisher sites take you to services with their own privacy policies, which this site does not control.

Email

If you write to the address on the contact page, that message is held in an institutional mailbox at Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences and is used only to reply to you.
