Assistant Professor of Civil Engineering

I teach civil engineering. I research how we can build better.

I am Dr. S. Vincent Sam Jebadurai. I teach at Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences, where I also work in institution relations. My research began with the behaviour and strengthening of masonry structures and has grown into work on 3D concrete printing, construction materials, digital construction and structural monitoring.

Who I am

My main work is teaching and research.

I am a civil engineering academic. I teach structural engineering subjects, guide student and doctoral research, review scholarly work and continue to work on construction problems that have practical value.

My doctoral research focused on masonry infill in reinforced-concrete frames. That led to experimental work on strengthening and, later, to a granted patent. In recent years, my research has moved toward additive manufacturing and digital methods in construction.

What I work on

Three questions guide most of the research.

I am interested in problems where structural engineering, materials and construction technology meet.

How do structures behave?

My earlier research examined masonry infills, reinforced-concrete frames, cyclic loading and seismic strengthening.

Research profile

How can construction change?

My more recent work includes 3D concrete printing, printable cementitious materials, reinforcement integration and construction automation.

Current projects

How can we inspect it better?

Recent publications explore computer vision, deep learning and digital methods for structural and infrastructure assessment.
