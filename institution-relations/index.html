The work

A pathway is something you build, not something you advertise.

Institution Relations brings together school engagement, academic outreach and institutional partnerships, with a focus on creating meaningful pathways between learners and higher education.

Much of school outreach is transactional: a stall, a brochure, a visit. What actually moves a sixteen-year-old toward engineering is doing something that feels like engineering, with people who do it for a living. That is the principle the work is organised around.

Case study

MINDSPARK: Flag Hunters

Challenge

School students rarely encounter university-level technical thinking before they have already chosen a stream. By the time outreach reaches them, the decision is largely made.

Idea

Reach them through something they already do voluntarily. A capture-the-flag format turns technical problem-solving into a game with a scoreboard, and it runs online, so participation is not limited by distance.

Execution

MINDSPARK: Flag Hunters was conceived and organised in 2026 as an online technical hackathon connecting school students with the University through game-based learning.

Outcome

The programme established a repeatable online format for school engagement that does not depend on campus visits. Participation figures will be published here once the organisers have confirmed them.

Partnership

What this work can lead to.

Schools looking for technical enrichment their own staff can run afterwards. Institutions looking for academic collaboration, joint programmes or faculty development. Industry looking for a route into student projects and applied research.

Each of those is a different conversation, and all of them start the same way.
