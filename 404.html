About

I trained as a structural engineer. What I have spent seventeen years actually building is a good deal less tangible than that.

Academia

Structural analysis, strength of materials and mechanics of solids are what every later technology rests on. Seventeen years of teaching has not changed that.

Research

The work began with masonry walls failing inside concrete frames. It now runs through printable materials, reinforcement integration and the digital methods used to inspect what gets built.

Innovation

Granted Indian patents, published applications and a filed system, each arising from a problem met in the laboratory rather than from a search for something to patent.

Publications

Masonry and seismic behaviour early, printable materials and applied machine learning recently. Titles, author order and journal names are reproduced exactly as published.

Leadership

As IQAC Coordinator he contributed to institutional quality-assurance efforts culminating in NAAC A++ accreditation with a CGPA of 3.53.

Speaking

Webinars for practising engineers, faculty development for teachers, and technical sessions for students. Each engagement is listed with its actual format.

Ministry

My work in ministry has deepened my understanding of leadership as service. Knowledge can develop the mind; service is what reaches people and communities.
