Crack characterisation in buildings utilizing deep learning techniques

What this paper is about

2025Przegląd ElektrotechnicznyData, AI and interdisciplinary research

This paper brings computer vision into building inspection. Instead of relying only on manual visual assessment, it explores deep-learning techniques for identifying and characterising cracks in buildings.

Authors: Jerushan, J., Jebadurai, S.V.S., Staines, N.J., Subin, S.R., Ebenezer, V., Ebenezer, S.A. and Hemalatha, G.,
